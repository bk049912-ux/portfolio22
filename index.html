Information Technology is one of the most powerful tools for achieving the Sustainable Development Goals in India. From AI and cloud computing to digital healthcare, education, agriculture and financial inclusion, technology can accelerate sustainable development.

Technology in India

India has developed large-scale digital platforms that demonstrate how technology can support development.

💳 UPI

Unified Payments Interface has transformed digital payments and supports financial inclusion by allowing fast digital transactions.

🏥 CoWIN & Digital Health

Digital health platforms demonstrate how technology can coordinate health services and provide citizens with digital access.

🎓 DIKSHA

Digital education platforms provide learning resources to students and teachers and support education beyond the classroom.

🌾 AgriTech

Satellite technology, IoT, weather information and data analytics can improve agricultural planning and productivity.

🏙️ Smart Cities

Sensors, GIS, digital infrastructure and analytics can help cities manage transport, water, waste and public services.

🛰️ Remote Sensing

Satellite data can support environmental monitoring, agriculture, disaster management and natural-resource planning.

Roadmap to 2030

India can maximise the benefits of digital technology by focusing on access, skills, innovation and responsible implementation.

01 • CONNECT

Expand affordable and reliable internet and digital infrastructure.

02 • EDUCATE

Develop digital literacy, coding, AI and cybersecurity skills.

03 • INNOVATE

Use AI, IoT, cloud computing and data analytics responsibly.

04 • MEASURE

Use data to measure progress and continuously improve development.

Technology for a Sustainable India 🇮🇳

Information Technology can become a powerful accelerator for sustainable development when it is affordable, secure, ethical, accessible and designed around real human needs.
